Tatoeba example sentences - Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR)

Sentences and English translations from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), exported November 2026.
Each sentence keeps its Tatoeba number; the studio links to it (https://tatoeba.org/en/sentences/show/<number>).
Used in East Asian Languages Studio: data/ex/zh.json, ja.json and ko.json, and the frequency estimates.
Changes made: Mandarin, Japanese and Korean sentences of up to 60 characters with an English translation were kept
(up to 60,000 Mandarin, 60,000 Japanese and 20,000 Korean), paired with their shortest English translation.
