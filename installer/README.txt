East Asian Languages Studio 1.0.3 - by Larry Dalton

Write, read and study Mandarin Chinese, Japanese and Korean on your own computer.
- Write Simplified or Traditional Chinese with pinyin or zhuyin, Japanese with furigana or romaji,
  Korean with romanization or Hanja; horizontal or vertical; English names by sound
- Dictionaries: CC-CEDICT, JMdict, Korean Wiktionary (+ kengdic); stroke order; example sentences
- English to Mandarin, Japanese and Korean drafts (rule-based, with a word-by-word breakdown)
- Export SVG, PNG and JPEG and read them back

Install: extract this folder, then double-click Install.cmd.
The studio installs for your Windows account only (no administrator rights), runs at http://localhost:8770/
and starts automatically when you sign in. The infographic is copied to your Desktop.

Uninstall: run Uninstall.ps1 from %LOCALAPPDATA%\EastAsianLanguagesStudio, or use Settings > Apps if you used the setup program.
